## Normalization counterexamples

`Apparatus/NormalizationChecks.lean` proves that the two alternating rational
matrices with upper-right entries 1/2 and 3/2 have the same entries modulo
integers but different numerical Pfaffians. The same finite torsion-valued
pairing therefore does not select the numerical Pfaffian of every lift.
A normalized integral presentation or a properly defined determinant-line
construction can provide additional data; this counterexample does not
exclude those targets.

The module also exercises the existing assembly and tensor APIs on positive
inputs 2, 3, 5, 7 with orientation 1. The final assembly is 210. Reusing that
final value as the fifth tensor factor produces 44100. Supplying the
independent orientation as the fifth factor produces 210. The later
manuscript repair must distinguish that input from the already assembled
four-column output.
